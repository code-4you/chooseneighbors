What it does

  • Counselling
  • Things to do
  • Community ideas share
  • Choose your neighbours

The profile — the AI gathers all of it into a Profile for your organiser / counselor. In a real community, this is what an organizer would see on their board: who wants to do what, who needs a hand, who belongs near whom, what ideas people have for the community — offered by each person, not extracted from them.

It's the smallest honest version of ideas from always something to do, the living map, and the design services — one person, one profile, one imaginary organizer.

This is an example, not an organization — there is no real organizer reading these profiles (except me maybe sometimes).
