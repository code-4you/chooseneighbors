Perpetuation of affordable homes

Profit from sale of homes will go back to investors and the purchase value will go back into buying or building new homes. If homes are not paid for they are resold to others.
If the business plan fails property is sold and money is returned to you, or you can keep the homes.

Home / Land titles

 
Homes are to be in the owners name, with Choose Neighbours also on the ownership papers,
So when homes are swapped there is no sales or purchase tax, just one or more of the owners' names changes.

Council approval is not needed.


 

How moving home works

A map is made and they can choose available homes where to live and groups they want to live near.  People can see the names of each area that are named by the residents.

Homes are made available near all people, are made free in places people are more likely to choose. As one group expands this will cause other groups to get shuffled to make room for as a person lives away from one group to another a space becomes vacant.
