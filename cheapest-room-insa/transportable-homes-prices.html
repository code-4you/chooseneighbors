2What the price covers — and what it doesn't

Included in the building price

  • Steel frame, insulated EPS wall & roof panels, double-glazed windows and a lockable glass entry door.
  • Floor, LED lights, power points and a 240 V switchboard installed to Australian AS3000 standard.
  • For Expanders: fold-out roof gas struts and unfolding instructions; footing plans supplied.
  • Bathroom or kitchenette only where the model name says so (e.g. "with Bathroom").

NOT included — budget on top

  • Delivery — quoted per job; the estimates above are mine, not the supplier's.
  • Licensed electrician & plumber to connect power, water and waste on site.
  • Footings / site prep — a level base or piers; the building is craned or slid off the truck.
  • Trailer — only on a trailer is a unit treated as a movable dwelling (caravan); add ~$13,950 for the 24 m² dual-axle.
  • Add-ons — air-con (~$825–$1,390), hot water (~$1,065–$1,290), cladding (~$1,250).

3How this fits the plan

In the salvage / land-trust model, the charity holds the land and each resident needs a dry, lockable, insulated room they can move into fast. A landed cabin or pod at ~$12,750–$23,250, or a 24 m² fold-out at ~$22,100, is the "buy it ready-made" alternative to building a room from salvaged parts — useful as the first few visible, occupied homes while self-builds get going. Method & honest notes

  • Building prices are real, delivery is estimated. Each price was read from that product's page on 19 June 2026. The delivery figures are my own ballpark for SA — the supplier does not publish a flat delivery price and quotes it per job.
  • Photos are the supplier's. Each image is Express Portables' own listing photo (some are 3D renders), loaded here from their site and linked back to the product. If an image doesn't load it has been moved on their end — use the link.
  • Prices move. The site runs frequent discounts and updates prices often, so the figures here will drift — confirm before you rely on them.
  • "Cheapest building" ≠ cheapest to live in. Once you add delivery, an electrician, a plumber, footings and a trailer, a sub-$20k sticker can land closer to $25k–$35k ready to occupy.
  • Not Class 1a by default. Most of these are sold as portable/relocatable buildings, not Class 1a houses; on a trailer they count as a movable dwelling. What you can put where depends on your council — see the overview page.
